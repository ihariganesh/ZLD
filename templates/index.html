Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) Monitoring Architecture

This enterprise-grade IoT dashboard continuously tracks zero liquid discharge operations. Raw effluent is measured for pH neutralization and solids turbidity prior to membrane separation. Flow transmitters on the feed, permeate recovery, and concentrate reject lines enforce strict mass-balance conservation. When a flow discrepancy exceeding the configured 15% tolerance is registered, an incident is instantly logged and dispatched via SMTP alert to environmental regulators.
